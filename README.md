# Sunlit Path

An English learning web app for high-school students (Logic and Expression I), designed for students with physical and intellectual disabilities. Runs in the browser; no login and no data is sent to a server in this public version.

- Version: v0.24 public
- Levels: Junior high review (CEFR A1–A2), up to B1, and up to B2. C1–C2 are in preparation.
- Dictionary: CEFR A1–A2 (2,557 words), B1 (2,325 words) and B2 (2,552 words)
- Grammar focus: practice one grammar point at a time (present perfect, present perfect progressive). Each use (e.g. completion, experience, continuation) has its own lesson — meaning and form, model sentences (listen, read aloud, voice input, typing, handwriting), sentence building, a short check and your own sentence — followed by mixed practice, careful comparisons with similar grammar (past tense, present tense, been/gone, present progressive) and a final check with results for each use.
- "My theme": students build their own word book and route. Saved only on the device.

Note: The levels (A1–C2) are practice stages based on word lists such as CEFR-J. This app is not test preparation for Eiken or CEFR exams and does not certify a level.
